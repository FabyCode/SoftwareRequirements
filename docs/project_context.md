# Spl-it — Project Context

## Project Overview

Spl-it is a collaborative web application that makes sharing expenses easy for groups by tracking purchases, calculating each person's share, and showing exactly who owes who.

The platform is intended to eliminate financial friction in shared-household, travel, and social group scenarios.

Examples of group scenarios may include:

- Roommates
- Travelers
- Couples
- Families
- Student teams
- Event organizers
- Groups of friends

## Business Opportunity

Managing shared expenses across social, domestic, and professional circles is currently an informal, fragmented, and error-prone process.

At the same time, groups need a transparent, automated, and equitable way to track and settle costs.

Spl-it explores the opportunity to:

- Eliminate calculation inaccuracies and uneven splits.
- Reduce social and interpersonal friction.
- Decrease administrative overhead for primary payers.
- Automate expense logging and balance calculations.
- Provide immediate, transparent balance visibility.
- Calculate optimal debt-settlement paths to minimize total transactions.

## Current Functional Epics

The project currently considers the following functional areas.

### 1. User Identity & Access Management

Functionality related to registering, authenticating, and managing user profiles within the platform.

### 2. Group Workspace & Frictionless Onboarding

Functionality related to organizing shared living, travel, and social contexts into isolated shared spaces with zero-friction onboarding.

### 3. Expense Logging & Categorization

Functionality related to capturing financial transactions made on behalf of a group, including amounts, dates, payers, notes, and categories.

### 4. Calculation Engine & Debt Optimization

Functionality related to adaptable split rules and backend graph-optimization algorithms that calculate net balances and minimize total transactions.

### 5. External Settlement Bridge & Debt Resolution

Functionality related to settling outstanding balances via deep-link bridges to external peer-to-peer platforms and manual settlement logging.

### 6. Ledger Dashboards & Expense Analytics

Functionality related to financial transparency, historical records, real-time net balances, spending charts, and exportable financial reports.

### 7. Activity Tracking & Automated Reminders

Functionality related to shared activity timelines, in-app notifications, status alerts, and automated polite payment reminders.

### 8. Platform Administration & Data Governance

Functionality related to backend controls, system health monitoring, tenant governance, and data privacy compliance.
